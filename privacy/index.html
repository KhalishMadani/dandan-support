Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short: dandan has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to a server. Your camera and face data stay on your phone.

Camera and face tracking

dandan uses the front camera and ARKit face tracking to show makeup on your face live. This runs on your device in real time. Face data, face meshes and camera frames are not stored, not uploaded, and not used for marketing, advertising or data mining, in line with Apple's App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2(vi).

The camera can also photograph a makeup palette so you can pick a colour. That image is not saved or uploaded.

Photos

Photos you take in the app (the desired look and your result) stay in memory while you use the flow and are cleared when you finish. They leave the app only if you choose to share them or save them to your Photos library. dandan asks for permission only to add photos to your library.

Data stored on your device

dandan stores only app settings, for example that you have seen the welcome screens, in your device's settings storage (UserDefaults). This is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

dandan has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking. The app includes open-source libraries that run on your device.

Children

dandan does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
